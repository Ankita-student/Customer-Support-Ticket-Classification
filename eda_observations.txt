The dataset is well balanced: the largest category (Report, 52 tickets) is only 1.04x the size of the smallest (Login Issue, 50), so plain accuracy is a fair metric and no re-sampling is needed.
'Application Error' tickets are most often High priority (65% of them); 'Account Update' tickets are least urgent (10% High).
Overall 41% of tickets are Closed; the rest are still Open or In Progress.
Descriptions are short (on average 13.1 words), so every keyword matters for the model.
Each category has its own keywords (see below), which is why a bag-of-words / TF-IDF model is expected to work well.
Some categories share vocabulary (e.g. 'account' appears in both Login Issue and Account Update tickets, 'update' in Account Update and Billing Issue, and one Performance ticket mentions a 'report' page), so a few confusions are expected.
Filler words such as 'hi', 'help', 'support' and 'thanks' appear in every category; TF-IDF down-weights words that appear everywhere, so they should not mislead the model.

Top keywords per category:
Account Update: account, update, help, hi, support, need
Application Error: error, hi, application, help, page, support
Billing Issue: support, help, payment, hello, invoice, team
Login Issue: login, sign, password, help, support, hi
Performance: slow, help, application, kindly, resolve, soon
Report: report, help, need, hi, sales, team
